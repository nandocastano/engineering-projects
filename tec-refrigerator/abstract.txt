Multi-Stage Thermoelectric Refrigerator

Seven students designed, built and controlled a compressor-less cooler driven by Peltier (TEC1-12706) modules, an Arduino PID loop and PWM power electronics. The first build failed in two ways: the TEC hot sides saturated thermally and the switching MOSFETs overheated. We redesigned the thermal stack (a printed three-generation fan/TEC platform with PLA kept out of heat paths, cork frames, PU-foam sealing, a condensate collector), the power stage (IRF520 gate-drive analysis, two MOSFETs per TEC, a separate acrylic MOSFET box) and the wiring. We ran step-response trials of three PID gain orderings and settled on Kp 100, Ki 0.1, Kd 1000. The cooler pulled a 24.0 °C chamber down to 18.0 °C in a stable, fan-mixed condition (about 72 min); a 4 °C local minimum was seen near the cold plate. It did not reach the sub-10 °C goal. A code review found the original sketch swapped the PID setpoint/input arguments (a working reverse-acting trick with a side effect on the derivative term); a conventional reference sketch is included. No controlled comparison of print materials was run.

Juan Fernando Castaño with the Falcons team. ENGR-UH 3110, NYU Abu Dhabi, Spring 2025.
